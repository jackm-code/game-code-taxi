# Progression

The full tree is in the Claude Doc "Skill Tree: Taxi Game". This file holds the rules that are Confirmed.

## XP [Confirmed]

XP comes from a mix: completed fares, clean routes and smooth driving.

## Level pacing [Confirmed]

- Night 1: 3 levels, so the first real choice lands inside the first night.
- Nights 2 to 5: 2 levels a night.
- Night 6 onwards: 1 level a night.
- This is a starting point to tune after playtesting.
- The 16 levels are used up by night 10, which leaves about five of the 15 nights with nothing left to unlock. Left as is for now, and to be judged in playtesting. Those nights are for spending money on Cab cosmetics.

## Skill tree rules [Confirmed]

- One mandatory starter skill, unlocked after the tutorial. It licenses the player to take fares and opens the four tier 1 skills.
- Each branch is a straight line. Tier N needs tier N-1.
- No skill reset.
- The Motor's upgrades cost only slightly more fuel, so they stay worth taking.

## The Cab: personalisation [Confirmed]

- The Cab is the second "fun" branch, for personalisation, and it sits outside the skill tree.
- Cab cosmetics cost money only, not skill points.
- The first few are cheap and available from the start, so spending on the cab while paying off the finance is a real choice between the two.
- In free play, the player's own money pays for them.

## Cab items [Confirmed]

Four items, all cosmetic (names are working names):
- **Dashboard Trinkets:** hang items from the mirror and place them on the dash.
- **Cassette Deck:** play tapes through the cab stereo.
- **Livery:** choose a colour scheme or advert panel for the cab.
- **Custom Horn:** choose the sound of the horn.

Price order, cheapest to dearest, as listed above: [Proposed]. Dashboard Trinkets is the cheap starter item, available from the start.

## Branches [Proposed]

Four skill branches (The Knowledge, Smooth Driving, Passengers and Radio, The Motor), 16 skills plus the starter, so 16 levels fill the tree. Names and effects are in the Skill Tree doc.
