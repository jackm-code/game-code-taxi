# Gameplay Loop

## The three loops

- **Fare (seconds to minutes):** Take a call, read the signs, deliver the passenger, get paid.
- **Night (15 minutes):** Start the shift at 10pm, chain fares, last call about 2:45am, end at 3am, see the tally. [Confirmed]
- **Career (about 15 nights):** Pay off the £10,000 finance on the cab and spend XP on skills so each night runs better than the last. [Confirmed] The 15 nights is a starting point to tune after playtesting.

## One night [Confirmed]

1. Start the shift at 10pm.
2. Take fares from the radio. Fuel drains as you drive.
3. Last call at about 2:45am. The fare in progress can be finished, so it is a shift and not a timer.
4. At 3am the night ends.

A night cannot be failed. A bad night just earns less, and running out of fuel costs a small tow fee and ends the shift early. [Confirmed]

## Money choices [Confirmed]

While the cab is being paid off, the first few cheap Cab cosmetics are available from the start. Buying one is a choice between personalising the cab and paying off the finance.

## When the cab is paid off [Confirmed]

A short "the cab is yours" ending with a final tally, then free play. In free play, nights keep running, the money is the player's to keep, any skills not yet unlocked are still there to unlock, and the money pays for Cab cosmetics (see `09-progression.md`).

## End-of-night tally [Proposed]

Earnings, fuel spent, money to the finance, XP gained and any level-ups, shown every night so progress is always visible.

## Why it should pull people back [Proposed]

Taken from the research doc: a visible finance balance, a first real skill choice inside the first night, and a clear next unlock keep the next reward close.
