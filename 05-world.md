# World and Setting

## Time period and place [Confirmed]

1990s UK. No GPS. Navigation relies on a paper map and road signs. Jobs come by radio. The player drives a black cab.

## The city [Confirmed]

A fictional city that feels like London. It is not London itself, so there are no real boroughs or street names. Unnamed for now; a name can come later [Open].

## The city's look [Confirmed]

A London-style city at night. Rain on the streets, amber sodium streetlights, and industrial areas and warehouses on the edges of town.

## The night [Confirmed]

A night shift from 10pm to 3am. Play runs on a compressed in-game clock. Last call is about 2:45am, and the fare in progress can be finished.

## The cab finance [Confirmed]

The player drives a financed black cab and is working to own it outright. Money from fares pays off the finance, and the balance is visible to the player. This replaces the earlier gambling-debt framing, because it is calmer.

- The balance is £10,000. [Confirmed]
- It should take about 15 nights to clear, roughly £670 earned per night. [Confirmed] A starting point to tune after playtesting.
- There is no interest, deadline or collector. [Proposed]
- Who the finance is with: not needed for now. [Open]

## The player character [Confirmed]

The player is an unnamed driver with no set backstory for now. The earlier characters (Mark, the loan shark, the ambulance crew) and their story are archived in `archive/horror-story/`.
