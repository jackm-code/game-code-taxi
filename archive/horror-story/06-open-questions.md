# Open Questions

The basics are settled. Everything below is parked for the storyline workflow, which comes next.

1. **Appearance:** What do Mark, Daniel, Andrew and the loan shark look like?
2. **Loan shark's return:** Which phase does he come back in, what happens, and is it Mark's final fare?
3. **Passenger conversations:** Do Mark's answers in optional conversations change anything later, or are they mood only?
4. **The ending:** Does Daniel know the patient is Mark before the final moment, or only as it happens? Does the player control the final act? Do Daniel and Andrew survive? Fixed ending or branching?
5. **Organ logistics:** Who is the organ recipient at the hospital, and does it connect to the story?
6. **Phase order:** Who the player starts with in Phases 2 and 3. (Proposed: Mark first in Phase 3.)
7. **Fail states and progression:** What happens if the driver crashes or a patient dies, including what happens to the pledged cab? Is there money, scoring or progression?
8. **Cab and rig interaction:** How the two stories touch, and how Daniel and Andrew interact through the partition, including in the final scene.
9. **Who sets the black-market jobs:** Does the loan shark assign the dark-alley drops, or does Mark take them voluntarily to pay down the debt?
10. **Debt details:** The amount owed and the terms are deliberately vague for now. When and how do they get revealed?
11. **City name:** The city is unnamed for now. Does it get a name later?
12. **Mark's family:** Do they appear in the game or only get mentioned?
