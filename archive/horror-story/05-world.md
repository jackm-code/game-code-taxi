# World and Setting

## Time period and place [Confirmed]

1990s UK. No GPS. Navigation relies on physical maps and road signs. Communication is by radio. The cab is a black cab and the ambulance is a 1990s UK ambulance.

## The city [Confirmed]

A fictional city that feels like London. It is not London itself, so there are no real boroughs or street names. The city is unnamed for now; a name can come later.

## The city's look [Confirmed]

A London-style city at 3 AM. Rain on the streets, amber sodium streetlights, and industrial areas and warehouses on the edges of town.

## Degradation [Confirmed]

The streetlights go out gradually through the night. The amber glow fades from street to street as the collapse spreads.

## The collapse [Confirmed]

The city is breaking down slowly. The player never learns the cause directly. It comes up in conversation but not at length, and it stays open to interpretation.

## The black market and loan sharks [Confirmed]

Medical supplies are in desperate demand, and shops and pharmacies keep selling out. People either buy from the black market or pay extortionate pharmacy prices, borrowing from loan sharks to afford them. This is what the loan shark means when he says the collapse is "good for business." Mark's own debt is from gambling, not from the shortage.

## Radio and navigation [Confirmed]

The player reads road signs to reach destinations. Next jobs come in over the radio. Each playable character has their own dispatcher.

## Early-night details [Proposed]

- Shops open at the wrong times, with lights on at 3 AM and queues outside but no one going in or out. (These could be pharmacies and stores that have sold out, tying them to the collapse. Not yet approved.)
- Familiar high street signs with a word or two altered, or council notices pasted over others.
- People standing still at bus shelters and crossings, facing the road, not moving as the cab passes.
- Dead air on the radio between calls, and empty junctions that should be busy.
- A closed clinic or depot with a van outside and one light on in a window.

## Locations [Proposed categories]

- **Clinics:** Routine organ transport between local clinics.
- **Hospital:** Legitimate organ deliveries and the destination of ambulance runs.
- **Dark alleys:** Black-market drop-offs.
- **Accident sites:** Where the ambulance is sent.
- **The wrecked taxi:** The site of the final scene.
- **Industrial edges:** Warehouses and depots on the outskirts, in keeping with the city's look.

## Loops and geometry [Proposed]

As the night degrades, streets may loop. Signs may lead back to the same point. The idea is not yet confirmed.
