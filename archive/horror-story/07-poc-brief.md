# POC Brief: Opening Loan Shark Fare

**Goal:** A playable prototype of the opening scene only. Choose the engine and project setup yourself and explain the choice briefly.

## Build this

1. Fade in, seated in a black cab, in rain at night. A 1990s London-style city, unnamed. Amber sodium streetlights.
2. A tutorial shows the player how to drive. An arrow points to the first pickup.
3. The cab stops. A loan shark gets into the back seat and says "just drive."
4. A short, scripted drive. The loan shark speaks a monologue. The player cannot respond.
5. He ends the fare with "keep the change."
6. The passenger is visible in the rear-view mirror during the drive.

## Use placeholders for

- The loan shark's appearance (open)
- The monologue wording (not yet written; use placeholder dialogue with the right content: Mark's debt, and the collapse is "good for business" because people turn to the black market or loan sharks for medical supplies)
- Audio and art (blockout geometry and temporary sound are fine)

## Don't build

- The Daniel/ambulance perspective, the partition, or the radio
- Passenger conversations
- Streetlight degradation, loops, or any later-phase horror
- Fail states, scoring, or progression

## Tone

Slow-burn. This is a threat, not a jump scare. Keep the drive quiet.

`03-structure.md` (Opening section), `02-characters.md` (Mark, loan shark), `05-world.md`. Only [Confirmed] items are canon. Treat [Proposed] items as optional.

## Done when

The scene runs start to finish in one sitting, the tutorial arrow leads to the pickup, and the loan shark's ride ends with "keep the change."
