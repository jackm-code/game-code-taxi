# Structure: One Night, Two Perspectives

## Overall approach

- The horror starts slow and gets scarier over the night. **[Confirmed]**
- The game takes place over one night, with one phase per shift. **[Confirmed]**
- The game alternates between Mark (cab) and Daniel (ambulance) at predetermined points. **[Confirmed]**
- The two storylines converge at the end. **[Confirmed]**
- Target length is about 1 to 2 hours. **[Confirmed]**

## Perspective switches [Confirmed]

Each of the three phases has one stretch for Mark and one for Daniel, so there are six stretches in total (Phase 1 Mark, Phase 1 Daniel, Phase 2 Mark, and so on). At roughly 1 to 2 hours, that is about 10 to 20 minutes per stretch.

- Phase 1 starts with Mark, because the game opens on his loan shark fare.
- Who the player starts with in Phases 2 and 3 is [Open]. Proposed: Mark first in Phase 3, because his final fare causes the crash that Daniel is sent to. **[Proposed]**

## Opening: the loan shark fare [Confirmed]

1. The player fades in, seated in a black cab.
2. A tutorial shows the player how to drive, and an arrow points to the first pickup.
3. The cab stops. The loan shark gets in and says "just drive."
4. The short drive is scripted. The loan shark delivers a monologue and Mark has no opportunity to respond.
5. He ends the fare mockingly with "keep the change."

This is a short story-setting drive, not a full scare. The horror is in the threat. Mundane fares come after it.

**Monologue content [Confirmed]:** Mark's backstory, and a high-level sense of why the city is collapsing. The loan shark says it's good for business: desperate people need medical supplies, shops sell out, and people turn to the black market or borrow from loan sharks to pay inflated prices. He does not name the amount owed or the terms; those are left vague for now. The aim is to keep the player intrigued without giving much away.

## Phase 1: Early night (mundane and uncanny)

- **Cab:** The opening loan shark fare. **[Confirmed]** After it, mundane fares: late-night passengers, drunk commuters, routine organ transfers between local clinics. Small details are slightly off: passengers whispering, coolers humming. **[Proposed]**
- **Ambulance:** Basic medical calls (car crashes, minor injuries). The sliding door reveals ordinary emergency care. **[Proposed]**

## Phase 2: Mid night (degradation) [Proposed]

- **Cab:** Black-market organ drops in dark alleys. Fares stop speaking coherently. Looking in the mirror reveals physical distortion, or passengers staring motionless at the back of the driver's neck.
- **Ambulance:** Calls turn into bizarre trauma cases. Patients thrash on the gurney or show unnatural symptoms or violence when the partition door opens.

## Phase 3: Late night (convergence) [Proposed]

- **Cab:** The roads degrade. Street signs begin to lead in circles. Mark takes a final fare or delivery that ends in a catastrophic road incident.
- **Ambulance:** Daniel drives through a chaotic city to a serious vehicle accident and finds a wrecked taxi. Daniel stays in the cab; Andrew handles the injured driver alone **[Confirmed]**. How Daniel and Andrew interact in this scene, and how Daniel realises who the patient is, are **[Open]**.

## Ending [Confirmed in outline; details Open]

- Mark becomes the final patient in Daniel's ambulance. **[Confirmed]**
- Daniel stays in the cab; Andrew treats Mark. **[Confirmed]**
- Open: whether Daniel knows who the patient is before the final moment, whether Daniel (and Andrew) survive, whether the player controls the final act, and whether the ending is one fixed outcome or branches.
- Open: whether the loan shark's return fare is Mark's final fare.
