# Characters

## Playable character 1: Mark, the cab driver ("The Road")

**Role [Confirmed]:** Night black cab driver. Takes fares and also makes deliveries of people and packages.

**Age [Confirmed]:** 50s.

**Perspective [Confirmed]:** Exposed and open. Mark can see passengers in the rear-view mirror. Conversations with passengers are optional (the opening loan shark fare is the exception: see `03-structure.md`).

**Cargo [Confirmed]:** Passengers, organ coolers (some black market, some legitimately due at a hospital), and other black-market cases.

**Backstory [Confirmed]:** Mark was a black cab driver before the gambling started. The gambling was a slow, slippery slope that cost him his family over time. He still has contact with his family, but they have set a condition: pay off the debt and sort his life out, or they're through.

**Motivation [Confirmed]:** Mark is working off a gambling debt to a loan shark. He believes that paying it off will let him "get on with his life" and win his family back. His cab is pledged as security, but it doesn't cover the full debt (both he and the loan shark know this). The loan shark has threatened to go after his family if the debt isn't paid. The black-market drops are only part of the work he does to pay it off.

**Ending [Confirmed]:** Mark's final fare or delivery leads to a catastrophic road incident, and he becomes the final patient in Daniel's ambulance.

**Appearance [Open]:** Parked until later.

## Playable character 2: Daniel, the ambulance driver ("The Rig")

**Role [Confirmed]:** Playable as the ambulance driver. Receives calls over the radio.

**Name [Confirmed]:** Daniel.

**Age [Confirmed]:** 30s.

**Background [Confirmed]:** A paramedic before the collapse. He was promoted to ambulance driver because of staff shortages. He doesn't see it as a step up, but he still wants to help people.

**Perspective [Confirmed]:** Restricted. A sliding door between the cab and the back must be opened and closed by the player. When the door is closed, the view of the back is limited. Daniel can talk to Andrew through the partition.

**Calls [Confirmed]:** Calls come in over the radio, as with Mark. They begin as ordinary medical calls and become increasingly disturbing.

**In the ending [Confirmed]:** Daniel stays in the cab at the crash scene. Andrew handles the patient alone. How Daniel and Andrew interact in that scene is [Open].

**Appearance [Open]:** Parked until later.
